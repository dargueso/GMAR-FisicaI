"Centre → Edge" and "Edge → Edge" match the Session guide's merry-go-round example (R = 4 m). "Lead the throw" is an exact hit from the centre; from the edge it only gets close. "Relative to Ana" adds her own platform speed to the throw — only matters when she's on the rim. "Show disc-fixed path" draws the curved path onto the ground view, spinning with the platform — like a line scratched onto the spinning disc. Keyboard: ← / → step back/forward one frame, Space plays/pauses.
